The Return Case for a Disciplined Investor

Strip the AI narrative away and look at the unit economics.

Go2 has a real, profitable, 8-year staffing business with negative churn and a proprietary data asset no funded competitor can buy. The new software product turns that data into near-zero-marginal-cost intelligence and automation engagements.

You already concluded the structure prices like a license — judged at roughly 8.5-12x earnings. Jeff, Langberg walked you through the enterprise vision. This is the skeptic's version: the floor, not the dream.

01

The problem is structural, not hype.

Every AI tool on the market works on what a person explicitly hands it — the prompt, the ticket, the CRM record, the chat log.

None of them work from what people actually do all day.

The real recurring work worth automating lives in the invisible gap between what someone tells a tool and what they actually do across their screen. Go2 starts from the actual work.

  • We observe genuine workflow: keystrokes, app usage, URLs, idle time.
  • We reconstruct the real process from telemetry, not memory.
  • This is a difference of input data, not model cleverness.
02

Two revenue streams.
One already pays the bills.

Go2 is a workflow-intelligence and labor-to-software conversion company. A skeptic should hold our two revenue streams separately:

1. The Staffing Business (The Base)

Real, profitable, running today. A remote-staffing subscription where staff work on client systems. Pricing is roughly double the cost of the person.

2. The Software Product (The Upside)

The same telemetry built to match workers now feeds an insight engine. It tells operators exactly how to automate their back office, and ships working automations.

The second stream rides on top of an asset the first stream already built and paid for. That is the crux of the return case.

03

The edge is a data feed, not an algorithm.

Be skeptical of the model layer. Models are commodity, and the orchestration pipeline is replicable by a funded competitor in 6-12 months.

The defensible thing is the input. Think of it like a faster market feed.

Go2's edge is raw workflow telemetry—captured locally at the keystroke/URL/app level—that simply cannot be bought or scraped. It works because of that raw data.

07

The staffing base de-risks the whole thing.

The downside is not a zero. Underneath the software is a staffing business that ran through COVID, is profitable, and operates on a month-to-month subscription.

  • Economics: ~30% profit margin, negative churn over 5 years.
  • Expansion: Land ~2 people (~$7K/mo) → Ramp toward ~$40K/mo.
  • Scale: 10 logos have each done >$1M in total revenue.
  • Current State: Gross ~$700K-$800K/mo, ~break-even, small $10K-$20K/mo burn.

The Honest Conflict

The software is good enough to remove ~80% of the work the staffing business bills for. Cannibalization caused a recent revenue dip. The conflict is real—and it is exactly why the value is migrating to software.

09

Timing & Incumbent Conflict.

Why Now?

The pipeline only became technically possible in December 2025, when model context-window, reasoning, and tool-use capabilities crossed the required threshold. The data asset is years old; the ability to monetize it this way is months old.

Why not Microsoft?

The economics are hostile to incumbents.

  • Microsoft bills per seat.
  • Model providers bill per usage.

A product whose entire value is removing seats and optimizing away usage is directly opposed to how they make money. Go2 has no such conflict.

10

The enterprise path: Credible, not closed.

Langberg pitched the enterprise dream. Let me draw the line honestly: enterprise is a credible path, not closed proof. There is no signed enterprise contract or term sheet in this deck.

What is true today:

  • Enterprise conversations consistently convert into JV/acquisition discussions.
  • Deep talks with a large VC firm on hardening for enterprise compliance.
  • The software is SOC 2 compliant on the cloud product used with clients.

The deliberate choice is to win SMB first. You cannot harden for enterprise and run the SMB wedge at full speed simultaneously. SMB makes the underlying IP more valuable.
